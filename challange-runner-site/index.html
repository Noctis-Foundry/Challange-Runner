Inside the Runner.

A simple interface for organizing challenges, discovering games and playing together.

What makes it special?

Features.

Everything you need to turn your game backlog into a series of unpredictable challenges.

01 / RANDOMIZER

Random Games

Generate 1–5 random games from your Steam library. Let fate decide what you play next.

02 / MULTIPLAYER

Game Lobbies

Create or join lobbies and organize challenges with your friends.

03 / FILTERS

Custom Filters

Choose from 47 categories, 12 genres and release years to customize your selection.

04 / PROGRESS

Progress Tracking

Follow everyone's progress, current games, completion status and playtime.

05 / RULES

Challenge Rules

Configure roll counts, difficulty, multiplayer requirements and rewards.

06 / CROSS-PLATFORM

Play Anywhere

Built with Avalonia for a cross-platform experience on Windows and Linux.

Getting started

Four simple steps.

Connect Steam

Authorize through Steam and access your game library.

Find your party

Create a lobby or join your friends.

Roll the dice

Configure filters and generate random games.

Take the challenge

Play, complete games and track your progress.
